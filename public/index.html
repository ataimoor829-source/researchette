Free guides

Start learning today, free.

Write a research question with PICOThe starting point of every study

In [Population], does [Intervention], compared with [Comparison], affect [Outcome]?

Then check it is Feasible, Interesting, Novel, Ethical and Relevant (FINER).

Search PubMed like a proMeSH terms and Boolean operators
  • Use MeSH terms: "Anxiety"[Mesh]
  • OR for synonyms, AND for concepts: (anxiety OR stress) AND "medical students"
  • Filter to the last 5 years.
Vancouver referencingThe style most medical journals use

Example: Khan A, Ali S. Title of article. J Pak Med Assoc. 2023;73(4):812-6.

Number references in the order you cite them. List up to 6 authors, then “et al.”

Case report structureBased on the CARE guidelines
  1. Title, abstract and keywords
  2. Introduction
  3. Patient information and findings
  4. Timeline
  5. Diagnosis, treatment and outcome
  6. Discussion and learning points
  7. Patient consent
Letter to the editorA realistic first publication
  1. Cite the article you are responding to.
  2. Make one or two clear points.
  3. Support them with references.
  4. Keep it within 300–600 words.
Meta-analysis in 6 stepsWhat a PRISMA review involves
  1. Write a protocol and register it on PROSPERO.
  2. Search at least 3 databases.
  3. Screen studies with two reviewers.
  4. Extract data.
  5. Assess risk of bias.
  6. Pool results in RevMan and report with PRISMA.

FAQ

Good questions.

Will you write my paper for me?

No. You write it yourself and we correct every step. That keeps your work original, and you actually learn.

I’ve never done research. Can I join?

Yes. The roadmap starts from zero, even in first year.

Will I get published?

We guide you through journal choice, submission and reviewer replies. The final decision is the journal’s, but our mentored students have published.

How much time does it take?

About 30 to 60 minutes a day, at your own pace.
